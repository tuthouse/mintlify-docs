**\$AGNTS** is the token beneath the collection. It is not a separate product — it is the price of an AGNT, expressed so a market can trade it.

<Note>
\$AGNTS is **not deployed yet**. Everything on this page switches on with it.
</Note>

## Supply

**1,000,000,000 \$AGNTS**, fixed.

- **50%** fair launch on AGNT
- **50%** held by the tut™ AMM vault for swaps

\$AGNTS will be tradeable on AGNT from launch, through custom v4 pools.

## What a piece is worth at the vault

Every AGNT has a fixed \$AGNTS value set by its tier. The vault swaps in **both directions** — hand over \$AGNTS and take a piece out, or hand in a piece and take \$AGNTS.

| Tier | Swap amount |
|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 |
| Rare | 500,000 |
| Epic | 1,500,000 |
| Legendary | 3,000,000 |

Which means the vault will accept any piece at the fixed amount for its tier, while it holds \$AGNTS to pay.

## Vault fees

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Take a random piece out | 5% of the floor |
| Pick the exact piece | 15% of the floor |
| Sell a piece in | none — you get the full amount |
| Burn | none |

The fee is **one way**. Selling a piece into the vault always pays the full amount.

## Activation

Activation has its own cost, separate from the swap amount: **100,000 \$AGNTS — the same for every
tier.** Half is burned, half goes to treasury. A further **10% of that amount is paid in ETH** to
treasury.

| | |
|---|---|
| Cost | 100,000 \$AGNTS |
| Burned | 50,000 |
| Treasury | 50,000 |
| ETH fee | 10% of the \$AGNTS value |

**A Common and a Legendary pay exactly the same to switch on.** Tier decides your share of
distributions afterwards — it does not decide what activation costs.

Activation is a service: it turns the piece on. The work is the same whichever tier you hold, so
the price is the same. The fee needs no floor price and no oracle — nothing about it depends on
reading the market.

### What it costs in dollars

The \$AGNTS amount never changes. What changes is what \$AGNTS is worth. On the fixed supply of
1,000,000,000:

| Token market cap | \$AGNTS | Worth | ETH fee | Total to activate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| \$100k | 100,000 | \$10.00 | \$1.00 | **\$11.00** |
| \$250k | 100,000 | \$25.00 | \$2.50 | **\$27.50** |
| \$500k | 100,000 | \$50.00 | \$5.00 | **\$55.00** |
| \$1M | 100,000 | \$100.00 | \$10.00 | **\$110.00** |
| \$5M | 100,000 | \$500.00 | \$50.00 | **\$550.00** |

Same row for every tier — there is only one price.

<Note>
These are arbitrary token prices picked to show the arithmetic. They are not a forecast, a target,
or a suggestion that \$AGNTS reaches any of them. \$AGNTS is not deployed and has no price.
</Note>

### Staying affordable if the token moves

The cost is a fixed number of \$AGNTS, so if the token rises the dollar cost rises with it. The
protocol can lower the amount, and **can only ever lower it** — the setter refuses any change that
would raise the cost. Nobody can be charged more than the price they saw when they decided to buy.

The piece itself moves too. Activation stays worth doing as long as the collection's floor keeps
pace with the token, and both are driven by the same activity.

<Note>
Activation is a **cost**, not a reward. What you pay is burned or goes to treasury — none of it is
distributed to other holders.
</Note>

## It burns from both ends

The pieces burn on the ladder. The token burns on activation. Both draw from the same vault.

<Warning>
AGNTS are collectibles. Activation is a mechanic within the collection, not an investment, and nothing on this page should be read as a return, a yield, or a promise of any payment.
</Warning>
