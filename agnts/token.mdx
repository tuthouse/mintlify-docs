**$AGNTS** is the ERC-20 beneath the collection. It is not a separate product — it is the price of an AGNT, expressed so a market can trade it.

<Note>
$AGNTS is **not deployed yet**. Everything on this page switches on with it.
</Note>

## Supply

**1,000,000,000 $AGNTS**, fixed.

- **50%** fair launch on AGNT
- **50%** to the tut™ AMM vault, backing the pieces

Trades run through custom v4 pools, and your agent can buy autonomously at launch.

## The peg

Every AGNT has a fixed $AGNTS value by tier. The vault trades at it **both directions** — bring $AGNTS and take a piece out, bring a piece and take $AGNTS.

| Tier | Peg |
|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 |
| Rare | 500,000 |
| Epic | 1,500,000 |
| Legendary | 3,000,000 |

Which means every piece has a redemption value that **moves with the token**, rather than depending on someone bidding for that specific piece.

## Vault fees

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Take a random piece out | 5% of the floor |
| Pick the exact piece | 15% of the floor |
| Sell a piece in | none — full peg |
| Burn | none |

The fee is **one way**. Selling into the vault always returns the full peg.

## Activation

Activating burns **50% of the peg** and sends the other half to treasury. A 10% activation fee is distributed to holders who are already activated.

<Note>
The 50/50 split is decided but not yet built — the contract currently burns the full peg with no treasury leg.
</Note>

## It burns from both ends

The pieces burn — 5 Commons into a Rare, on up the ladder. The token burns — every activation destroys peg permanently.

Same vault backs both. One gets scarcer, the other gets scarcer, and they are priced against each other.
