**\$AGNTS** is the token beneath the collection. It is not a separate product — it is the price of an AGNT, expressed so a market can trade it.

<Note>
\$AGNTS is **not deployed yet**. Everything on this page switches on with it.
</Note>

## Supply

**1,000,000,000 \$AGNTS**, fixed.

- **50%** fair launch on AGNT
- **50%** held by the tut™ AMM vault for swaps

\$AGNTS will be tradeable on AGNT from launch, through custom v4 pools.

## What a piece is worth at the vault

Every AGNT has a fixed \$AGNTS value set by its tier. The vault swaps in **both directions** — hand over \$AGNTS and take a piece out, or hand in a piece and take \$AGNTS.

| Tier | Swap amount |
|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 |
| Rare | 500,000 |
| Epic | 1,500,000 |
| Legendary | 3,000,000 |

Which means the vault will accept any piece at the fixed amount for its tier, while it holds \$AGNTS to pay.

## Vault fees

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Take a random piece out | 5% of the floor |
| Pick the exact piece | 15% of the floor |
| Sell a piece in | none — you get the full amount |
| Burn | none |

The fee is **one way**. Selling a piece into the vault always pays the full amount.

## Activation

Activation has its own cost, separate from the swap amount — **100,000 \$AGNTS for a Common**, rising to 1,000,000 for a Legendary. Half is burned, half goes to treasury. A further **10% is paid in ETH** to treasury.

| Tier | \$AGNTS | Burned | Treasury |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 | 50,000 | 50,000 |
| Rare | 300,000 | 150,000 | 150,000 |
| Epic | 600,000 | 300,000 | 300,000 |
| Legendary | 1,000,000 | 500,000 | 500,000 |

**Plus a fee of 10% of the piece's floor price, paid in ETH** to treasury. Note what that is a percentage of — the floor, not the \$AGNTS cost.

### How the floor is worked out

The ETH fee is 10% of the floor — and the floor comes from the ladder, not from the market. **A Rare counts as five Commons, an Epic as fifteen, a Legendary as thirty**: the same counts it takes to burn your way up a rung. One observed price, the Common floor, sets all four.

That is deliberate. Above Common there is not enough trading to read a price from — a few dozen Epics and Legendaries sit in public hands, so the lowest listing is whatever one person happened to ask, with nothing underneath it to correct it. Working the floor out from the ladder instead means the fee cannot be moved by a single listing, and no price feed is needed to charge it.

At a Common floor of \$77.25:

| Tier | Counts as | Floor | ETH fee (10%) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1 Common | \$77.25 | \$7.73 |
| Rare | 5 Commons | \$386.25 | \$38.63 |
| Epic | 15 Commons | \$1,158.75 | \$115.88 |
| Legendary | 30 Commons | \$2,317.50 | \$231.75 |

### What it costs in dollars

The \$AGNTS amount never changes. What changes is what \$AGNTS is worth. Below is the same fee worked out at three different token prices, on the fixed supply of 1,000,000,000.

<Note>
These are three arbitrary token prices picked to show the arithmetic. They are not a forecast, a
target, or a suggestion that \$AGNTS reaches any of them. \$AGNTS is not deployed and has no price.
</Note>

**The two parts move differently, and that is the point of keeping them separate.** The \$AGNTS part is ten times larger for a Legendary than for a Common, and it rises and falls with the token. The ETH part is thirty times larger and does not move with the token at all, because it tracks the floor. At a \$100k market cap the ETH fee is about 70% of what a Legendary holder pays; at \$1M it is about 19%.

### When activation costs more than the piece

Because the \$AGNTS part rises with the token while the ETH part does not, every tier reaches a point where activating costs more than simply buying another piece at the floor:

| Tier | Activation matches the floor at |
|---|---|
| Common | **\$695,000** |
| Rare | \$1.16M |
| Epic | \$1.74M |
| Legendary | \$2.09M |

Commons reach that point first, and Commons are most of the collection. This is worth knowing before you activate anything. The activation cost is a number the protocol can lower — and only lower, never raise — which is the lever for it.

The swap amount is what the vault gives you for a piece. Activation is a separate fee. They are two different numbers and changing one does not change the other.

<Note>
Activation is a **cost**, not a reward. What you pay is burned or goes to treasury — none of it is distributed to other holders.
</Note>

<Note>
The 50/50 split is decided but not yet built — right now the whole amount is burned and none of it reaches the treasury.
</Note>

## It burns from both ends

The pieces burn on the ladder. The token burns on activation. Both draw from the same vault.

<Warning>
AGNTS are collectibles. Activation is a mechanic within the collection, not an investment, and nothing on this page should be read as a return, a yield, or a promise of any payment.
</Warning>
