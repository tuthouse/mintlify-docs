**$AGNTS** is the ERC-20 beneath the collection. It is not a separate product — it is the price of an AGNT, expressed so a market can trade it.

<Note>
$AGNTS is **not deployed yet**. Everything on this page switches on with it.
</Note>

## Supply

**1,000,000,000 $AGNTS**, fixed.

- **50%** fair launch on AGNT
- **50%** held by the tut™ AMM vault for swaps

$AGNTS will be tradeable on AGNT from launch, through custom v4 pools.

## The peg

Every AGNT has a fixed $AGNTS value by tier. The vault trades at it **both directions** — bring $AGNTS and take a piece out, bring a piece and take $AGNTS.

| Tier | Peg |
|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 |
| Rare | 500,000 |
| Epic | 1,500,000 |
| Legendary | 3,000,000 |

Which means the vault will accept any piece at the fixed amount for its tier, while it holds $AGNTS to pay.

## Vault fees

| | Fee |
|---|---|
| Take a random piece out | 5% of the floor |
| Pick the exact piece | 15% of the floor |
| Sell a piece in | none — full peg |
| Burn | none |

The fee is **one way**. Selling into the vault always returns the full peg.

## Activation

Activation has its own cost, separate from the peg — **100,000 $AGNTS for a Common**, rising to 1,000,000 for a Legendary. Half is burned, half goes to treasury. A further **10% is paid in ETH** to treasury.

| Tier | $AGNTS | Burned | Treasury |
|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 | 50,000 | 50,000 |
| Rare | 300,000 | 150,000 | 150,000 |
| Epic | 600,000 | 300,000 | 300,000 |
| Legendary | 1,000,000 | 500,000 | 500,000 |

**Plus a fee of 10% of the piece's floor price, paid in ETH** to treasury. Note what that is a percentage of — the floor, not the $AGNTS cost.

The peg is what the vault pays for a piece. Activation is a fee. They are different numbers and neither depends on the other.

<Note>
Activation is a **cost**, not a reward. What you pay is burned or goes to treasury — none of it is distributed to other holders.
</Note>

<Note>
The 50/50 split is decided but not yet built — the contract currently burns the full peg with no treasury leg.
</Note>

## It burns from both ends

The pieces burn on the ladder. The token burns on activation. Both draw from the same vault.

<Warning>
AGNTS are collectibles. Activation is a mechanic within the collection, not an investment, and nothing on this page should be read as a return, a yield, or a promise of any payment.
</Warning>
