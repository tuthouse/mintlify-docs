**AGNTS** is a collection of 6,969 agents, every trait hand-drawn by tut™. No AI.

All 6,969 pieces — art, traits and tier — were generated and committed **before** the mint opened. Nothing was decided at mint time, and `tierOf(tokenId)` is readable by any RPC client. The collection mixes hand-drawn crayon work with hidden pixel-art AGNTS on a 13×13 grid.

The cap is **6,969 and immutable**. Nothing mints after genesis, so every path through the economy either holds supply flat or destroys it. The collection gets smaller the more it is used.

## The four tiers

| Tier | Supply | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 5,669 | 81.3% |
| Rare | 1,000 | 14.3% |
| Epic | 250 | 3.6% |
| Legendary | 50 | 0.7% |

Two numbers govern everything: the **peg**, which is the fixed amount the vault swaps a piece for, and the **weight**, which sets an activated piece's share of a distribution. They are deliberately different.

## Where things stand

<Note>
**Live:** the collection is minted and trading on secondary at a 5% royalty.

**Not open yet:** minting is paused, burning is disabled, and nothing has been burned. The $AGNTS token is not deployed, so the vault, activation and rewards are all waiting on it.
</Note>
