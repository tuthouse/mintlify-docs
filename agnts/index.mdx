**AGNTS** is a collection of 6,969 agents, every trait hand-drawn by tut™. No AI.

Every piece — its art, its traits and its tier — was finished and locked in **before** the mint opened. Nothing was decided while the mint was running, and anyone can check any piece's tier directly on the blockchain. The collection mixes hand-drawn crayon work with hidden pixel-art AGNTS on a 13×13 grid.

The cap is **6,969 and can never change**. No new pieces are ever made, so everything that happens either leaves the count alone or lowers it. The collection only gets smaller.

## The four tiers

| Tier | Supply | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 5,669 | 81.3% |
| Rare | 1,000 | 14.3% |
| Epic | 250 | 3.6% |
| Legendary | 50 | 0.7% |

Two numbers drive everything: the **swap amount**, which is what the vault gives you for a piece, and the **weight**, which sets how big a share an activated piece gets of a payout. They are deliberately different.

## Where things stand

<Note>
**Live:** the collection is minted and trading on secondary at a 5% royalty.

**Not open yet:** minting is paused, burning is disabled, and nothing has been burned. The \$AGNTS token is not deployed, so the vault, activation and rewards are all waiting on it.
</Note>
