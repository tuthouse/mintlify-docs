---
title: "Burning & the Ladder"
description: "Burn a stack, draw a piece from the vault at the next tier up, with odds to land higher."
---

Burning is how holders move pieces up a tier, and how the collection shrinks. Burn a stack from **one wallet** and you get a random piece back from the vault, one tier up, **with a chance of landing higher still**.

<Note>
Burning is **not switched on yet**. Nothing has been burned. Everything below is the locked design, not something you can do today.
</Note>

## The ladder

| Burn | Get |
|---|---|
| 5 Common | A Rare or better |
| 3 Rare | An Epic or better |
| 2 Epic | A Legendary |

**No fee.** Burning does not pay the vault-use fee.

<Warning>
**Burning ends an activation.** If any piece in the stack was switched on, it is destroyed along
with the piece, and unclaimed amounts are forfeited. The piece you draw comes back **inactive** —
switching it on is a fresh cost. **Claim first, then burn**, and consider climbing to the tier you
intend to keep before activating anything.
</Warning>

You cannot combine pieces across wallets — one wallet has to hold the whole stack. Legendary is the top of the ladder and never burns.

## The odds

| Burn | Draw |
|---|---|
| 5 Common | **96%** Rare · **3%** Epic · **1%** Legendary |
| 3 Rare | **90%** Epic · **10%** Legendary |
| 2 Epic | **100%** Legendary |

Every Rare burn pays an Epic or better. There are no sideways draws on any rung — you never burn a stack and get the same tier back.

## What the vault holds back

The odds are set so most of the upper tiers never reach the market through burning. What stays behind goes to swap packs.

| Tier | In the vault | Out via burns | Reserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rare | 930 | ~494 | ~436 |
| Epic | 221 | ~84 | ~137 |
| Legendary | 46 | ~37 | ~9 |

<Tip>
The odds can be changed later. But they control **flow, not stock** — pieces already out of the vault cannot be recalled.
</Tip>

## Where burning ends

The vault releases Common into circulation to hold roughly half the live supply, so burning keeps feeding itself until the stacks run out.

| | Start | End |
|---|---|---|
| Supply | 6,969 | ~4,115 |
| Destroyed | 0 | ~2,854 |
| Burns | 0 | ~615 |

Every Legendary that comes out is out early — by the third round of seven.

Modelled against the 1,306 wallets that actually hold pieces today. **968 of them hold a single piece and can never burn**, so every burn comes from the other 338.
