Activated AGNTS earn a share of the protocol's fees, **paid in USDG** straight to the wallet that holds each piece. Rewards run in monthly **seasons**, and each payout is split across activated pieces by tier weight.

<Note>
**Season 0 is live.** 11,000 USDG is paying out over 30 days, Oct 5 → Nov 4 2026, to every AGNT that was activated when Season 0 closed (Oct 5, midnight EDT) and is still activated.
</Note>

## Seasons

Each season pays out a fixed pot evenly over 30 days. Fees earned during one season build the next.

| Season | Pot | Runs | Who earns |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Season 0** | 11,000 USDG | Oct 5 → Nov 4 | AGNTS activated before Oct 5, midnight EDT (04:00 UTC) |
| **Season 1** | 2,014 USDG carried over + 70% of every fee from Oct 5 on | Nov 4 → Dec 4 | Activated AGNTS |

Season 0 pays out what holders earned before it launched. **Activate now and you earn from Season 1.**

## Weight by tier

| Tier | Weight | Over the rung below |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 1× | — |
| Rare | 6× | +20% |
| Epic | 21× | +17% |
| Legendary | 55× | +31% |

The weights run steeper than the tier ratios. Five Commons stake 5; one Rare stakes 6. A Rare's weight (6) is higher than the combined weight of the five Commons burned to draw it (5), and the same holds at every rung. Otherwise a burn would leave your share unchanged.

## How your share is worked out

Your share is your weight divided by the total weight of everything earning in the season.

```text
your share  =  your weight  ÷  total activated weight
```

Season 0 has a total weight of 2,706. A Legendary (55) earns 55 ÷ 2,706 of the season's 366.67 USDG a day — about 7.45 USDG a day. A Common (1) earns about 0.14 USDG a day.

Shares move only as pieces leave: when one sells or deactivates, the rest of the season is split across everyone still earning.

## How you get paid

- **Automatically, every day.** A keeper pays out once at least 0.05 USDG has built up. You don't need to claim.
- **To the holder.** Payouts go to the wallet that holds the NFT — never to the agent's own wallet. Anyone can trigger a payout, but it can only ever go to the holder.
- **On chain.** The rewards contract on Robinhood Chain is `0x649e378764d2131Ad7f1bcC7EEDAbbC8A9406840`.

## Selling, moving and deactivating

**Sell or move an activated piece and it stops earning.** The collection counts every time a piece changes hands. The moment it leaves your wallet, anything it earned since its last payout is forfeited to everyone still activated. What was already paid out is yours.

- **Buying an activated piece buys you nothing.** It arrives switched off. Activate it yourself and it earns from the next season.
- **Deactivate** and you're paid everything earned up to the last hourly check-in, then it stops.

Burning works the same way. The pieces you burn are destroyed along with their activation, and the piece you draw from the vault comes back inactive — see [Burning &amp; the Ladder](/agnts/burns).

## Where the fees come from

70% of these fee streams goes to holders. The other 30% goes to the protocol treasury.

| Stream | What's counted |
|---|---|
| Secondary sales | 5% royalty on every sale |
| Activations | the ETH activation fee |
| Vault swaps | the swap fee (5% random, 15% pick your own) |
| Degen swaps | the fee only |
| \$AGNTS buy engine | buy and sell fees |
| Curated launches | the protocol's 0.4% — from Season 1 |

The \$AGNTS part of the activation cost is not counted: half is burned and half goes to treasury. Separately, **1.095% on \$AGNTS pool trades goes 100% to the protocol** and none of it enters the pool. Burns pay no fee.

## Season 0 \$AGNTS bonus

On top of the USDG, every AGNT activated for Season 0 gets a one-time \$AGNTS bonus:

| Tier | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Common | 50,000 \$AGNTS |
| Rare | 100,000 \$AGNTS |
| Epic | 200,000 \$AGNTS |
| Legendary | 400,000 \$AGNTS |

- **It unlocks in full on Nov 4.** Nothing is paid before then; the whole bonus is paid at once, to the wallet that holds the piece.
- **Hold and stay activated for the 30 days.** Sell, move or deactivate the piece before Nov 4 and its bonus goes back to the pool.
- **One bonus per NFT, ever.**

## What affects your share

- **Your tier** — a higher tier carries more weight
- **How many others earn** — more activated weight means a smaller slice each
- **Protocol activity** — each season's pot is whatever fees came in

<Note>
What a future season pays depends entirely on how much activity there was. There is no fixed rate, no guaranteed amount and no promised outcome — if there is no activity, there is nothing to distribute. Nothing here is a projection or a forecast.
</Note>

<Warning>
AGNTS are collectibles. Activation is a mechanic within the collection, not an investment, and nothing on this page should be read as a return, a yield, or a promise of any payment.
</Warning>
