Activating an AGNT puts it to work and makes it eligible for a share of protocol fees. Rewards come from one shared pool, split by stake weight.

<Note>
Activation is a **cost**, not a reward stream. What you pay to activate is burned or goes to treasury — none of it is paid out to other holders. See [$AGNTS &amp; the Vault](/agnts/token) for what it costs.
</Note>

<Note>
Rewards are **not live**. They switch on with the $AGNTS token. The weights below are a proposal, not final.
</Note>

## Weight by tier

| Tier | Weight | Over the rung below |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 1× | — |
| Rare | 6× | +20% |
| Epic | 21× | +17% |
| Legendary | 55× | +31% |

The weights run steeper than the tier ratios on purpose. Five Commons stake 5; one Rare stakes 6. **Climbing earns more than the stack it cost, every rung** — otherwise burning four pieces would change nothing.

Each tier's share of the pool is *derived* — activated count × weight, over the total — and re-derived **weekly**. It is never a fixed percentage set at launch.

## Where the fees come from

**70% of fees go to holders, 30% to the protocol.**

| Stream | Rate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Secondary sales | 5% | Live |
| Curated launches | 0.4% | Live |
| Vault swaps — random | 5% | Needs the token |
| Vault swaps — pick your own | 15% | Needs the token |

| RWA distributions | — | Needs the bound wallet |

Separately, **1.095% on $AGNTS trades goes 100% to the protocol** with no holder split. Burns pay no fee at all.
