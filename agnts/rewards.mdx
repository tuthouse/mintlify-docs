title: "Rewards"
description: "How activation works and how tier weight decides your share of distributions."
---

Activating an AGNT puts it to work and makes it eligible for a share of protocol fees. Rewards come from one shared pool, split by stake weight.

<Note>
Activation is a **cost**, not a reward stream. What you pay to activate is burned or goes to treasury — none of it is paid out to other holders. See [$AGNTS &amp; the Vault](/agnts/token) for what it costs.
</Note>

<Note>
Rewards are **not live**. They switch on with the $AGNTS token. The weights below are a proposal, not final.
</Note>

## Weight by tier

| Tier | Weight | Over the rung below |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 1× | — |
| Rare | 6× | +20% |
| Epic | 21× | +17% |
| Legendary | 55× | +31% |

The weights run steeper than the tier ratios on purpose. Five Commons stake 5; one Rare stakes 6. **A Rare carries more weight than the five Commons it cost**, and the same holds at every rung — otherwise burning four pieces would change nothing about your share.

Each tier's share of the pool is *derived* — activated count × weight, over the total — and re-derived **weekly**. It is never a fixed percentage set at launch.

## Where the fees come from

**70% of fees go to holders, 30% to the protocol.**

| Stream | Rate | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Secondary sales | 5% | Live |
| Curated launches | 0.4% | Live |
| Vault swaps — random | 5% | Needs the token |
| Vault swaps — pick your own | 15% | Needs the token |

| RWA distributions | — | Needs the bound wallet |

Separately, **1.095% on $AGNTS trades goes 100% to the protocol** with no holder split. Burns pay no fee at all.

## How your share is worked out

Your share of a distribution is your weight divided by the total weight of everything activated.

```text
your share  =  your weight  ÷  total activated weight
```

Nothing more to it. If you hold an activated Epic and the total activated weight across the
collection is 1,699, your share is 21 ÷ 1,699 — about 1.2% of whatever is distributed that epoch.

### Worked example

Say these are activated:

| Tier | Activated | Weight each | Total weight | Share of the pool |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | 1,169 | 1× | 1,169 | 69% |
| Rare | 28 | 6× | 168 | 10% |
| Epic | 12 | 21× | 252 | 15% |
| Legendary | 2 | 55× | 110 | 6% |
| | | | **1,699** | **100%** |

Commons take the largest share of the pool because there are far more of them, while a single
Legendary carries 55× the weight of a single Common.

These shares are **derived, not fixed** — they move as pieces are activated and deactivated, and
they are recalculated every epoch.

<Note>
Fees distributed depend entirely on protocol activity in that epoch. There is no fixed rate, no
guaranteed amount, and no promised outcome — if there is no activity, there is nothing to
distribute. Nothing here is a projection or a forecast.
</Note>

## What affects your share

- **Your tier** — a higher tier carries more weight
- **How many others activate** — more activated weight means a smaller slice each
- **Protocol activity** — the pool is whatever fees came in

<Warning>
AGNTS are collectibles. Activation is a mechanic within the collection, not an investment, and
nothing on this page should be read as a return, a yield, or a promise of any payment.
</Warning>
