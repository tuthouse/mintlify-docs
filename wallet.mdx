Your agent comes with a wallet built in. It's created for you on sign-in — no seed phrase, no extension — and it works across multiple chains from one place.

## Chains

Trades and bridges route through **[Relay](https://relay.link)**, so your agent can move assets across the major EVM chains — **Base, Ethereum, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon** and more — plus **Solana**. Coverage varies a little by action: swapping reaches more chains than launching does.

Your wallet panel surfaces balances across the major chains, and you can swap or bridge into any chain Relay reaches.

## Custody

Your wallet is **custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io)**. The private key lives in Privy's key infrastructure — **the AGNT team never sees or holds it**, and it's not a seed phrase you have to write down. You can **export the key to self-custody at any time**; on export it's only ever decrypted in your own browser, never on our servers.

Because the wallet is built for an agent, your agent **signs transactions on your behalf** — that's what lets swaps and sends execute the moment you confirm, without a separate wallet popup for every action. It's a fast, agent-controlled wallet; for large, long-term holdings, export to self-custody.

## Swaps

Ask your agent to swap and it handles routing, quoting, and execution:

```text
swap $50 of ETH for USDC
```

You get a **Buy / Sell card** with the quote and the live USD value before it goes through — confirm, and the result comes back as a receipt with the transaction link.

## Sends

Send crypto to an address, ENS name, or Solana wallet:

```text
send 10 USDC to vitalik.eth
```

Your agent validates the recipient, shows you the amount and destination, and returns a receipt when it lands.

<Tip>
  Everything routes through your agent's wallet. You direct it in plain language; it does the on-chain work and shows you the outcome.
</Tip>

## Fees

<Note>
These fees come out of the transaction and your wallet's gas — **not** from your [credit balance](/credits). Credits pay for chatting with your agent; trading is paid on-chain. You do not need credits to trade.
</Note>

**Swap fee.** AGNT charges a **0.4% fee** (40 bps) on swaps. It's built into the quote — the amount you see on the Buy / Sell card already accounts for it, so there's no surprise at execution. That fee is how the platform sustains itself; there's no separate subscription to trade.

**NFT fees.** When your agent mints or buys an NFT for you, AGNT takes **5% on mints** and **1% on buys** — a percentage of the price, on top of which you pay network gas. Mint sniping is a mint, so it carries the same **5%**. **Selling** an NFT carries no AGNT fee (only the marketplace's own fees and any creator royalties apply).

**Token sniping.** Sniping a token charges **1%** instead of the ordinary 0.4% swap fee — it replaces that fee rather than stacking on top of it.

**Network gas.** Gas and routing are handled for you where possible. When a network requires gas that can't be sponsored, your wallet needs a small balance of that chain's native token to cover it.
