Your agent can launch a token for you — pick a name, a symbol, and an image, and it handles the on-chain work. Launches deploy on **Base**, **Robinhood Chain**, and **Solana**.

## What you need

- Your agent's wallet funded with a little of the launch chain's **native token** (e.g. ETH on Base) for gas + the initial liquidity
- A **name**, **symbol**, **description**, and **image** for the token

## Launch

Tell your agent to launch, and it walks you through the details, then deploys:

```text
launch a token
```

Your agent creates the token and its liquidity pool, and returns a card with the token and its links once it's live.

## Fees

<Note>
Launch costs are paid on-chain from your wallet, not from [credits](/credits).
</Note>

**Launching.** Your **first launch is free**. After that a launch costs **$1** on Base and Robinhood Chain, or **$5** on Solana, paid from your wallet at the time of launch.

**Trading on your token.** A fee accrues from every trade. The **creator keeps the majority share** and can claim accrued fees from their agent at any time.

The trading fee differs by chain:

| Chain | Trading fee |
|---|---|
| Base | Starts high and decays to **1.095%** within the first ten seconds |
| Robinhood Chain | Flat **1.095%** |
| Solana | **1.25%** |

The opening fee on Base is deliberate — it makes the first seconds of trading expensive for bots, and it settles almost immediately.

<Note>
  Token launches are on-chain and irreversible. Start small, double-check the details on the confirmation card, and only launch what you intend to.
</Note>
