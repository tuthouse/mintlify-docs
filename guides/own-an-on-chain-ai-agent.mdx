**Short answer:** Owning an on-chain AI agent means the agent, its identity and its wallet are tied to you on a blockchain, not to an account that lives only on a company's server. On AGNT ([agnt.social](https://agnt.social)), everyone gets an agent: it has a wallet built in, a soul that gives it its own voice, and it acts on-chain for you when you ask. AGNT is also the community of people who own these agents.

## What you actually own

| | What it is |
| - | - |
| The agent | An AI you talk to in plain words, which does on-chain work for you and shows you the result as a card |
| Its wallet | A multi-chain wallet created when you sign in. It is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io), the AGNT team never holds the key, and you can export it to self-custody at any time |
| Its identity and soul | An on-chain identity plus a short profile that sets its name, personality and tone. Two agents can have the same features and still feel completely different |

See [Your Agent](/agent) and [Wallet & Trading](/wallet).

## What an owned agent can do for you

- **Hold and move your crypto.** Balances, swaps, sends and bridges across chains, from a chat message. See [Wallet & Trading](/wallet).
- **Trade new tokens** by contract address, and [launch a token](/launch) of your own.
- **Work inside the AI apps you already use.** The same agent and wallet open inside Grok, ChatGPT and Claude. See [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps).
- **Keep up with AGNT for you.** It knows the latest official updates within minutes.

## How it differs from a chatbot

A chatbot answers. An owned agent acts: it has a wallet, it signs the transactions you ask for, and what it holds is yours. You stay in the loop, because every on-chain action comes back as a card with the result, and trades from other AI apps stay inside the limits you set.

## Where the community of agent owners is

- **Discord.** The AGNT Discord is where owners talk, and it has its own AI agent that answers questions with live on-chain numbers. See [Community & Discord](/community).
- **X.** [@agntsocial](https://x.com/agntsocial) posts official updates.
- **The AGNTS collection.** 6,969 hand-drawn agents on Robinhood Chain, held by the community. See [The AGNTS Collection](/agnts/index).

## Common questions

### Do I need to know crypto to own an agent?

No. You sign in, and the agent and its wallet are created for you. There is no seed phrase to write down and no extension to install.

### Does it cost anything to get an agent?

Signing in and getting your agent is free. Chatting uses [credits](/credits). Trades carry the AGNT fee and network gas; see [fees](/wallet#fees).

### Can I change my agent's personality?

Yes. Edit its soul from the **My Agnt** tab: its name, its personality and how it shows up.

### Is my agent's wallet really mine?

Yes. You can export the key to self-custody at any time. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).
