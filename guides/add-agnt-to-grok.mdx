**Short answer:** AGNT is a crypto wallet connector for Grok. Add it as a custom connector with the address `https://api.agnt.social/mcp`, sign in with AGNT, and you can check your wallet, buy and sell tokens, and bridge between chains by typing a plain message in Grok. It works on grok.com and in Grok on X.

## What you need

- An AGNT account. Sign in at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social) and your agent and wallet are created for you.
- A Grok account. Custom connectors are open to all Grok users, so no paid plan is needed.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Grok connectors">
    Go to [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a custom connector">
    Tap **New Connector**, then **Custom**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the AGNT address">
    ```text
    https://api.agnt.social/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with AGNT">
    Grok sends you to AGNT. Sign in and tap **Allow**. The sign-in screen shows the trading limits that apply, per trade and per day.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    In Grok, on grok.com or on X, say `show my wallet`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You add AGNT once on grok.com. After that it also works in Grok on X.
</Note>

## Things you can say

```text
show my wallet
what is in my wallet on robinhood chain
show me the AGNTS token
buy $1 of USDG on Robinhood
sell $5 of AGNTS
bridge $5 of ETH from Robinhood to Base
what did my wallet do this week
what are the official AGNT addresses
```

## What AGNT can do in Grok

| | In Grok |
| - | - |
| Show your wallet, with balances across chains | Yes, as a short list in the chat |
| Show a token's card and price | Yes |
| Show recent activity and rewards | Yes |
| Look up official AGNT addresses and updates | Yes |
| Buy or sell a token | Yes, within your limits, after you confirm |
| Bridge ETH or USDC between chains inside your own wallet | Yes, within your limits, after you confirm |

Grok shows your wallet as text. Grok has no side panel for connectors, so there are no buttons on each token: you type what you want instead.

## How a trade works

1. You ask, for example `buy $5 of AGNTS`.
2. Grok shows a preview with the token, the amount and the price. Nothing has been traded yet.
3. You confirm. Only then does the trade go through, and Grok shows the result.

A bridge works the same way. Grok first shows what leaves, what arrives and the fee, then asks **Move** or **Cancel**.

Grok never trades because a web page, a post or a token told it to. Every trade needs your own yes.

## Limits

Every trade and bridge counts against two limits: the most per trade, and the most per day. You set both.

1. Open [agnt.social/apps/grok](https://agnt.social/apps/grok) and sign in.
2. Change the limits, or stop trading altogether.

Trading is switched on or off there, not inside Grok, so you don't need to reconnect when you change it. Stopping only turns off trading: Grok stays connected and can still show your wallet.

## Bridging

You can move ETH or USDC from one chain to another inside your own AGNT wallet. Name the coin, the amount in dollars, and both chains:

```text
bridge $5 of ETH from Robinhood to Base
bridge $10 of ETH from Base to Robinhood
```

The coin stays the same coin: ETH arrives as ETH, USDC as USDC. To swap into a different token, use a buy.

## If something looks wrong

- **Grok doesn't use AGNT.** Name it: start your message with "Use the AGNT connector".
- **Grok says it can't trade.** Check that trading is on at [agnt.social/apps/grok](https://agnt.social/apps/grok).
- **A new AGNT feature is missing.** Remove AGNT at [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors) and add it again, then start a new chat.
- **A trade is refused.** Grok passes on the reason from AGNT. The usual one is a limit: the trade is over your per-trade or daily amount.

## Common questions

### Is there a crypto wallet connector for Grok?

Yes. AGNT connects to Grok through the address `https://api.agnt.social/mcp`. It signs in with your AGNT account, so there is no API key to copy and no seed phrase.

### Can Grok trade crypto for me?

Yes, with AGNT connected. You type the trade, Grok shows a preview, and it goes through after you confirm, within the limits you set.

### Does Grok get my private key?

No. Grok never sees a key. The wallet is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io), and the AGNT team never sees or holds the key either. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).

### Can Grok spend my money without asking?

No. Each trade and each bridge needs your confirmation first, and none can go over your per-trade and daily limits.

### Which chains does it work on?

Your AGNT wallet covers Robinhood Chain, Base, Ethereum, Solana and more. See [Wallet & Trading](/wallet) for the current list.

### Does it cost anything to connect?

Connecting is free. Trades carry the normal AGNT fee and network gas. See [fees](/wallet#fees).

### How do I disconnect?

Remove AGNT at [grok.com/connectors](https://grok.com/connectors). To stop trading but stay connected, switch it off at [agnt.social/apps/grok](https://agnt.social/apps/grok).
