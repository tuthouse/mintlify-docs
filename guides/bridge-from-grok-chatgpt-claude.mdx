**Short answer:** With AGNT connected, you can bridge ETH or USDC between chains from inside Grok, ChatGPT or Claude. In Grok you type it, for example `bridge $5 of ETH from Robinhood to Base`, and confirm. In ChatGPT and Claude the AGNT wallet panel opens and you tap **Move**. The coin moves between chains inside your own AGNT wallet; nothing goes to another person.

## What you can bridge

| Coin | Between these chains |
| - | - |
| ETH | Ethereum, Base, Robinhood Chain, Arbitrum, Optimism |
| USDC | Base, Ethereum, Polygon, Arbitrum, Solana |

The coin stays the same coin: ETH arrives as ETH, USDC as USDC. To end up with a different token, use a buy instead.

## How it works in each app

| | How you bridge | What confirms it |
| - | - | - |
| Grok | Type the move in the chat | Grok shows what leaves, what arrives and the fee, then you answer **Move** or **Cancel** |
| ChatGPT | Type the move, or tap **bridge** in the AGNT panel | The panel opens with the move filled in. You tap **Move** |
| Claude | Ask for it, or tap **bridge** in the AGNT panel | The panel opens. You tap **Move** |

In every app, nothing moves until you confirm it yourself.

## Things you can say

```text
bridge $5 of ETH from Robinhood to Base
bridge $10 of ETH from Base to Robinhood
move $20 of USDC from Base to Solana
bridge $25 of USDC from Ethereum to Arbitrum
```

## What it costs

A little less arrives than leaves: the AGNT fee and the bridge's own fee come out of the amount. The preview shows both sides before you confirm. Current rates are in [Wallet & Trading](/wallet#fees).

## Limits

A bridge counts against the same two limits as a buy: the most per move, and the most per day. You set both for each AI app at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps), and you can switch trading off there at any time.

## Common questions

### Can Grok bridge crypto for me?

Yes, with AGNT connected. You type the move, Grok shows a preview, and it goes through after you answer **Move**.

### Can ChatGPT bridge crypto for me?

With AGNT connected, ChatGPT opens the AGNT wallet panel with the move filled in. You tap **Move** in the panel. ChatGPT itself never moves the funds.

### Can Claude bridge crypto for me?

With AGNT connected, Claude opens the AGNT wallet panel, and you tap **bridge** and then **Move**. Claude itself may decline to place the move in chat; the panel is the way to do it.

### Where does the money go?

To the same wallet on the other chain. A bridge here is always from your own AGNT wallet to your own AGNT wallet.

### What if I name a coin or chain that can't be bridged?

AGNT says so and nothing is moved. Only ETH and USDC bridge this way, on the chains in the table above.

### Does the AI app get my private key?

No. The wallet is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io), and neither the AI app nor the AGNT team sees or holds the key. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).
