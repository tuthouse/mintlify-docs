**Short answer:** AGNT ([agnt.social](https://agnt.social)) lets you swap tokens on Robinhood Chain by typing a plain message, such as `swap $50 of ETH for AGNTS`. Every AGNT account comes with an AI agent and a built-in wallet that works on Robinhood Chain, Base, Ethereum and Solana. You say what you want, your agent does the on-chain work, and you get a card with the result.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [agnt.social](https://agnt.social) and sign in. Your agent and its wallet are created for you. There is no seed phrase to write down and no extension to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the wallet">
    Put some ETH on Robinhood Chain in your agent's wallet. Send it in from another wallet, or ask your agent to bridge it from a chain where you already hold funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the swap">
    Say what you want in plain words. You can name the token or paste its contract address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm on the card">
    Your agent shows a **Buy / Sell card** with the quote and the live USD value. Confirm, and the result comes back as a receipt with the transaction link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things you can say

```text
swap $50 of ETH for AGNTS on robinhood chain
buy $20 of 0x2c7a596448f329e3ad35181be4be19d941a682b9
sell half of my AGNTS
bridge 0.05 ETH from base to robinhood chain
what is in my wallet on robinhood chain
```

<Tip>
  Trade by **contract address** when you can. Token names are easy to copy; an address is not.
</Tip>

## Where you can do it

- **In AGNT chat** at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social).
- **Inside the AI apps you already use.** The same agent and wallet open inside ChatGPT, Claude and Grok. Setup for each one is at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps).

## What it costs

The AGNT fee is built into the quote, so the amount on the card is what you get. Network gas on Robinhood Chain is paid in ETH from your wallet. Chatting uses [credits](/credits); swapping does not. Current rates are in [Wallet & Trading](/wallet#fees).

## Robinhood Chain on AGNT

- Robinhood Chain's chain id is **4663**.
- Your agent's wallet holds ETH and tokens on Robinhood Chain next to your balances on Base, Ethereum and Solana.
- You can [launch a token](/launch) on Robinhood Chain from the same chat.
- The [AGNTS collection](/agnts/index) and the [\$AGNTS token](/agnts/token) live on Robinhood Chain.

## Common questions

### Do I need MetaMask or another wallet to swap on Robinhood Chain?

No. The wallet is built into your agent and is created when you sign in.

### Who holds the keys?

The wallet is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io). The AGNT team never sees or holds the private key, and you can export it to self-custody at any time. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).

### Does the AI trade without asking me?

A swap in chat shows you a card with the quote first. It runs when you confirm.

### Which tokens can I swap?

Tokens that have a route on Robinhood Chain, by name or by contract address. If there is no route for a token, your agent tells you it can't fill the trade and nothing is sent.

### Can I move my funds out?

Yes. Ask your agent to send to any address, or to bridge to another chain.
