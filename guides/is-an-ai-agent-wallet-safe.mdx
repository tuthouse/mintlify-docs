title: "Is it safe to let an AI agent hold your crypto?"
description: "What protects your funds when an AI agent has a wallet: who holds the key, what the agent can do without you, the limits you set, and what to keep elsewhere."
---

**Short answer:** It depends on four things: who holds the key, what the agent may do without asking you, what limits apply, and whether you can leave. On AGNT ([agnt.social](https://agnt.social)) the key is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io) and never held by the AGNT team, a trade shows you a card before it runs, trades from other AI apps stay inside limits you set, and you can export the key to self-custody at any time. An agent wallet is built for speed. Keep large, long-term holdings in self-custody.

## The four questions to ask of any agent wallet

| Question | On AGNT |
| - | - |
| Who holds the private key? | [Privy](https://privy.io) custodies it. The AGNT team never sees or holds it |
| Can I take the key and leave? | Yes. Export it to self-custody at any time. On export it is only decrypted in your own browser |
| Does the agent act without asking? | A swap or send in chat shows a card with the amount and destination first. It runs when you confirm |
| Is there a cap on what it can spend? | Yes for AI apps. Each connected app (Grok, ChatGPT, Claude) has a per-trade limit and a daily limit that you set, and a switch to turn trading off |

See [Custody](/wallet#custody).

## What protects you on AGNT

- **No seed phrase to lose or leak.** The wallet is created when you sign in.
- **A card before the trade.** You see the quote and the live USD value, then confirm.
- **Limits per AI app.** Set them, or switch trading off, at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps). Switching off does not disconnect the app; it can still show your wallet.
- **Your own yes, every time.** In Grok, a trade needs your confirmation after a preview. In ChatGPT and Claude, you tap the button in the AGNT wallet panel yourself.
- **Scam checks on buys.** Buys of tokens AGNT flags as scams are refused, and on Elysium a token is refused when every pool it has charges more than 10% per trade.
- **Look-alike tokens are kept apart.** A token posing as another is listed separately and is not counted in your wallet total.

## What an agent wallet does not protect you from

- **A bad trade you confirmed.** If you buy a token that goes to zero, that is the market, not the wallet.
- **Sending to the wrong address.** A send cannot be undone. Read the whole address before you confirm.
- **Look-alike tokens you choose to buy.** Trade by contract address and check it against the project's own official links.
- **Anyone who asks for your key.** The AGNT team never DMs you first and never asks for a seed phrase, a private key, or to "verify" or "sync" your wallet. See [Stay safe](/community#stay-safe).

## How much to keep in an agent wallet

Treat it like the cash you carry, not your savings. An agent wallet signs for you so trades and sends go through the moment you confirm. That speed is the point. For large, long-term holdings, export to self-custody.

## Common questions

### Can the AI drain my wallet?

A trade or send needs your confirmation, and trades from connected AI apps cannot go over the per-trade and daily limits you set.

### Can a web page or a token trick the AI into trading?

Each trade needs your own yes. A page, a post or a token cannot supply it.

### What happens if AGNT goes away?

You can export your key to self-custody at any time, and the funds are on public blockchains.

### Does ChatGPT, Claude or Grok get my private key?

No. The AI app never sees a key.
