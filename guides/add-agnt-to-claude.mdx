title: "Add AGNT to Claude"
description: "Connect your AGNT wallet to Claude with the AGNT connector: see your wallet, token cards and activity inside a Claude chat."
---

**Short answer:** AGNT is a crypto wallet connector for Claude. Add it as a custom connector with the address `https://api.agnt.social/mcp`, sign in with AGNT, and Claude can show your wallet, token cards and recent activity in the chat. The wallet appears as an AGNT panel with **Buy** and **Sell** buttons that you tap yourself.

## What you need

- An AGNT account. Sign in at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social) and your agent and wallet are created for you.
- A Claude account at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). If you don't see **Add custom connector** in step 2, your Claude plan doesn't include custom connectors.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your plugins in Claude">
    In Claude, open **Plugins** in the sidebar, or go to [claude.ai/plugins/yours](https://claude.ai/plugins/yours).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Add custom connector">
    Tap **Add**, then **Add custom connector**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the two boxes">
    **Name:** `AGNT`

    **MCP server URL:**

    ```text
    https://api.agnt.social/mcp
    ```

    Tap **Continue** and finish the second step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with AGNT">
    Claude sends you to AGNT. Sign in and tap **Allow**. AGNT then shows under **Connectors** in your plugins as a custom connector on `api.agnt.social`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Start a new chat and say `show my AGNT wallet`. The first time, Claude asks whether it may use AGNT: choose **Allow once** or **Always allow**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Claude labels AGNT a custom connector, because you added it yourself rather than from Claude's directory. That is expected.
</Note>

## What AGNT can do in Claude

| | In Claude |
| - | - |
| Show your wallet, with balances across chains | Yes |
| Show a token's card and price | Yes |
| Show recent activity and rewards | Yes |
| Look up official AGNT addresses and updates | Yes |
| Buy or sell from the AGNT panel, by tapping the buttons yourself | Yes, within limits you set |
| Claude buying, selling or sending for you by itself | No |

**Claude is read only.** Claude itself never buys, sells or sends for you. That is Claude's own rule, and no setting in AGNT changes it. Trades in Claude happen only when you tap **Buy** or **Sell** in the AGNT panel.

## Trading from the panel

The buttons are off until you switch them on:

1. Open [agnt.social/apps/claude](https://agnt.social/apps/claude) and sign in.
2. Set the most you allow per trade and per day.
3. Tap **Let Claude trade**.

If that page says trading isn't open for your account yet, everything else on this page still works.

You can change the limits or stop trading there at any time. Stopping only turns off trading: Claude stays connected and can still show your wallet.

## If something looks wrong

- **Claude doesn't use AGNT.** Name it: start your message with "Use the AGNT connector".
- **A new AGNT feature is missing.** In your plugins, open the **...** menu next to AGNT and choose **Refresh tools list**, then start a new chat.
- **The wallet panel doesn't open.** Start a new chat and ask again. If Claude asks for permission, allow it.

## Common questions

### Is there a crypto wallet connector for Claude?

Yes. AGNT connects to Claude through the address `https://api.agnt.social/mcp`. It signs in with your AGNT account, so there is no API key to copy.

### Does Claude get my private key?

No. Claude never sees a key. The wallet is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io), and the AGNT team never sees or holds the key either. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).

### Can Claude spend my money?

Not by itself. Claude refuses to place trades or sends. A trade only happens when you tap a button in the AGNT panel, and only within the limits you set.

### Does it cost anything to connect?

Connecting is free. Trades carry the normal AGNT fee and network gas. See [fees](/wallet#fees).

### How do I disconnect?

Remove AGNT from your plugins in Claude. To stop trading but stay connected, switch it off at [agnt.social/apps/claude](https://agnt.social/apps/claude).
