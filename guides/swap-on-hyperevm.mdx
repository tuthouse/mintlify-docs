**Short answer:** AGNT ([agnt.social](https://agnt.social)) lets you swap tokens on HyperEVM by typing a plain message, such as `swap $50 of HYPE for USDC on hyperevm`. Every AGNT account comes with an AI agent and a built-in wallet that reaches HyperEVM. You say what you want, your agent does the on-chain work, and you get a card with the result.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [agnt.social](https://agnt.social) and sign in. Your agent and its wallet are created for you. There is no seed phrase to write down and no extension to install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the wallet">
    Put some HYPE on HyperEVM in your agent's wallet. Send it in from another wallet, or ask your agent to bridge it from a chain where you already hold funds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type the swap">
    Say what you want in plain words. You can name the token or paste its contract address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm on the card">
    Your agent shows a **Buy / Sell card** with the quote and the live USD value. Confirm, and the result comes back as a receipt with the transaction link.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things you can say

```text
swap $50 of HYPE for USDC on hyperevm
buy $20 of <token address> on hyperevm
bridge 0.1 HYPE from hyperevm to elysium
what is in my wallet on hyperevm
```

<Tip>
  Trade by **contract address** when you can. Token names are easy to copy; an address is not.
</Tip>

## Where you can do it

- **In AGNT chat** at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social).
- **Inside the AI apps you already use.** The same agent and wallet open inside ChatGPT, Claude and Grok. Setup for each one is at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps).

## What it costs

The AGNT fee is built into the quote, so the amount on the card is what you get. Network gas on HyperEVM is paid in HYPE from your wallet. Chatting uses [credits](/credits); swapping does not. Current rates are in [Wallet & Trading](/wallet#fees).

## HyperEVM on AGNT

- HyperEVM's chain id is **999**.
- Gas on HyperEVM is paid in **HYPE**.
- Swaps and bridges on HyperEVM route through [Relay](https://relay.link).
- From HyperEVM you can bridge HYPE straight to [Elysium](/guides/swap-on-elysium).

## Common questions

### Do I need MetaMask or another wallet?

No. The wallet is built into your agent and is created when you sign in. There is no seed phrase to write down and no extension to install.

### Who holds the keys?

The wallet is custodied by [Privy](https://privy.io). The AGNT team never sees or holds the private key, and you can export it to self-custody at any time. See [Custody](/wallet#custody).

### Does the AI trade without asking me?

A swap in chat shows you a card with the quote first. It runs when you confirm.

### Which tokens can I swap on HyperEVM?

Tokens that Relay can route on HyperEVM, by name or by contract address. If there is no route for a token, your agent tells you it can't fill the trade and nothing is sent.

### Can I move my funds out?

Yes. Ask your agent to send to an address, or to bridge to another chain.
