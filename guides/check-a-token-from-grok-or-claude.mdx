**Short answer:** With AGNT connected, you can ask Grok or Claude about any token by name or contract address and get its price, 24-hour chart, market cap, liquidity, volume, holders and safety checks. It works for tokens on Robinhood Chain, Base, Solana and Ethereum. Looking up a token moves no money.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an AGNT account">
    Sign in at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social). Your agent and its wallet are created for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect AGNT">
    Follow the steps for [Grok](/guides/add-agnt-to-grok) or [Claude](/guides/add-agnt-to-claude). The connector address is the same for both:

    ```text
    https://api.agnt.social/mcp
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask about a token">
    Name it, or paste its contract address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Things you can say

```text
show me the AGNTS token
what is the price of 0x2c7a596448f329e3ad35181be4be19d941a682b9 on robinhood
analyze this token on base: <token address>
is <token address> on solana safe to buy
show my wallet
which AGNTS do I hold
```

## What you get back

| | What it shows |
| - | - |
| Price | The current price and its 24-hour chart |
| Size | Market cap, fully diluted value, liquidity and volume |
| Activity | Buys and sells, and the number of holders |
| Safety | Safety checks and how concentrated the holders are |

<Tip>
  Ask by **contract address** when you can. Many tokens share a name; an address points at exactly one.
</Tip>

## More you can look up

- **Your wallet.** Balances across your chains, as a list in Grok and as a wallet panel in Claude.
- **Your AGNTS.** The pieces of the [AGNTS collection](/agnts/index) your wallet holds, with each one's tier and whether it is activated.
- **Recent activity and rewards** for your wallet.
- **Official AGNT addresses and updates.**

## Common questions

### Can Grok check a token's price for me?

Yes, with AGNT connected. Ask for the token by name or contract address and Grok shows its card.

### Can Claude check a token's price for me?

Yes, with AGNT connected. Ask for the token by name or contract address.

### Does this cost anything?

A lookup moves no money and does not count against your trading limits. Only a buy, sell, bridge or send moves money.

### Which chains does it cover?

Token lookups cover Robinhood Chain, Base, Solana and Ethereum.

### Can I buy the token after I look at it?

Yes. In Grok, type the buy and confirm the preview. In Claude, use the **Buy** button in the AGNT wallet panel. Both stay inside the limits you set at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps).

Sign in at [agnt.social](https://agnt.social), connect AGNT at [agnt.social/apps](https://agnt.social/apps), and ask about your first token.
