The AGNT Discord has its own AI agent, built on AGNT. It answers the questions people ask every day, so you get an answer right away instead of waiting on a mod.

## What it can do

- **Answer questions** about AGNT, the AGNTS collection, `$AGNTS`, burning, activation, and using the app.
- **Post official contract addresses.** Ask `ca?` or "what's the `$AGNTS` ca". It only ever posts the official ones.
- **Look up prices.** Ask "price of AGNTS" or "`$ETH` price".
- **Give live on-chain numbers**, read from the chain minutes ago:
  - how many AGNTS have been burned
  - how many pieces are activated
  - what's sitting in the AGNT vault, by tier
  - which burns are open right now, and which are out of stock
- **Share the latest news.** It learns from what tut posts in the official announcement channels, so it's always current.

## How to use it

Ask in any channel. The bot answers on its own when it's sure it has the right answer, and stays quiet otherwise.

To ask it directly, **tag it**. When it doesn't have a confirmed answer, it tags the mods for you.

<Tip>
  Short, direct questions work best: "how do I activate?", "can I burn epics right now?", "how many are burned?"
</Tip>

## Stay safe

<Warning>
  The bot and the AGNT team **never DM you first**, and **never ask for a seed phrase, private key, or to "verify" or "sync" your wallet**. Anyone who does is a scammer.
</Warning>

- The bot only links to official AGNT sites: [agnt.social](https://agnt.social), [docs.agnt.social](https://docs.agnt.social), [mint.agnt.social](https://mint.agnt.social), and [@agntsocial on X](https://x.com/agntsocial).
- It never shares passcodes, never predicts prices, and never gives financial advice.
- If a message tells you to claim, mint, or connect your wallet somewhere else, it isn't from AGNT.

## One source of truth

The Discord AI and [your agent on agnt.social](/agent#it-knows-whats-new) learn from the same official updates. Whether you ask in Discord or in the app, you get the same answer.
