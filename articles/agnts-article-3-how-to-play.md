### How to get involved

Four steps, and only the first one is available today.

1. **Get a piece.** Secondary is open.
2. **Climb, if you want to.** Burn a stack, draw higher. *(Not open yet.)*
3. **Switch it on.** Activation makes a piece eligible for distributions. *(Needs $AGNTS.)*
4. **Hold it.** Activation lives as long as you do — sell it and it switches off.

### Activating

**This is the part that matters, and the part people skip.**

An unactivated AGNT is art. An activated one is eligible for a share of what the protocol
collects. Switching it on costs $AGNTS — half of which is **burned** — plus a fee in ETH.

| Tier | $AGNTS | ETH fee |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 100,000 | 10% of floor |
| Rare | 300,000 | 10% of floor |
| Epic | 600,000 | 10% of floor |
| Legendary | 1,000,000 | 10% of floor |

Three things to know before you do it, because two of them cost people money:

- **Activation does not survive a sale.** Sell the piece and it switches off, and anything
  unclaimed is forfeited. **Claim before you sell.** Buying an activated piece buys you
  nothing — it arrives off.
- **Activation does not survive a burn.** Climb the ladder and the activation dies with the
  pieces you burned. **Climb to the tier you intend to keep, then activate.**
- **The cost only ever goes down.** It is a settable number that cannot be raised. Nobody gets
  charged more than the price they saw.

### Rewards

Activated pieces split **70%** of the fees the protocol collects. The other 30% goes to
treasury. Split by weight, recalculated weekly.

| Tier | Weight |
|---|---|
| Common | 1× |
| Rare | 6× |
| Epic | 21× |
| Legendary | 55× |

The weights run steeper than the tiers on purpose. Five Commons stake 5; the Rare they burn
into stakes 6. **Climbing has to leave you better off than standing still, or the ladder is
decoration.**

What funds it: 5% on AGNTS secondary sales, 0.4% on curated launches, and 5%/15% on vault
swaps. Both of the first two are collecting **today**.

Nothing has been distributed yet. What gets paid depends entirely on activity — no fixed rate,
no guaranteed amount. If there is no activity there is nothing to split.

### $AGNTS

1,000,000,000 tokens, fixed. Half fair-launched, half held by the vault to swap against pieces.

**$AGNTS is what you spend to use the collection** — activating a piece, or buying one out of
the vault. That is the whole of its job.

Be clear about what it is not: **holding $AGNTS does not pay you.** Distributions go to
activated pieces, not to token holders. The 1.095% taken on $AGNTS trades goes to the protocol
and never enters the pool.

And there is a hard ceiling worth knowing. Activating every piece once would cost
**1,066,900,000 $AGNTS against a 1,000,000,000 supply** — more than exists. Half of every
activation is burned, so activating destroys the means to activate. **The collection can never
be fully switched on.** That is by construction, and it is why the cost ratchets down.
