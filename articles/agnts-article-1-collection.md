## Rarity — three paths

1. **Pull it.** Open a mint pack — any tier can come out.
2. **Snipe it.** Take a specific AGNT from the vault, at a premium.
3. **Burn for it.** Climb the ladder below.

## The Burns

Burns are how holders climb tiers — and how the collection shrinks.

Burn a stack, and you get a **random AGNT from the vault in the next tier up** — with odds to land higher than that.

🔥 5 Commons → a Rare or better
🔥 3 Rares → an Epic or better
🔥 2 Epics → a Legendary

Every burn:

- Moves your piece up a tier
- Permanently removes the burned pieces from supply
- Modelled against today's holders, supply ends near **4,100** once burning runs out. That end state is a projection.

**Burns are not open yet.** The ladder ratios are locked; the burn phase switches on after mint settles. Zero AGNTS have been burned to date — that is verifiable on chain.

## Rewards

Every tut™ AGNT is a functional AI agent in the AGNT protocol. An activated AGNT is eligible for the protocol's fee distribution.

When distributions go live, the pool will be funded by **70%** of the fee streams listed below and nothing else. The remaining **30%** goes to the tut/AGNT treasury. Nothing has been distributed to date.

Those fees come from:

- **5%** on secondary sales and vault swaps
- **10%** activation fee
- **0.4%** on curated token launches after mint

Rarity tier will set an activated piece's distribution weight: **Common 1× · Rare 6× · Epic 21× · Legendary 55×**. Distributions are not live. Nothing has been distributed.

The weights are steeper than the burn ratios on purpose: a Rare's weight (6) is higher than the combined weight of the five Commons burned to draw it (5). Otherwise a burn would leave your share unchanged.
