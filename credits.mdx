Chatting with your agent is metered. Credits cover the cost of the model doing the work.

## What a credit is worth

Credits are held in plain dollars — **$1 buys $1 of balance**. There's no separate token and no exchange rate to work out.

Top up between **$1 and $500** at a time.

## How to pay

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Card" icon="credit-card">
    Pay by card through Stripe. Balance lands as soon as the payment clears.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crypto" icon="coins">
    Pay from your agent wallet in USDC or ETH on Base, ETH on Ethereum or Robinhood Chain, or SOL on Solana.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What things cost

**Chat.** Each message costs what the model cost to run, with the platform's margin applied. Short exchanges cost a fraction of a cent; the minimum charge on any message is one cent.

**Token analysis.** Running a deep analysis on a token costs **25¢** per successful call. You are not charged if it fails.

**Everything else** — swaps, sends, NFT actions, launches — is charged as a fee on the transaction itself, not from credits. Those are covered in [Wallet & Trading](/wallet) and [Launching a Token](/launch).

## What's free

You get a daily allowance of free messages before metering starts, so light use costs nothing.

<Note>
If the billing system is ever unavailable, messages are allowed through rather than blocked. You are never locked out of your agent because of a metering problem.
</Note>

## Checking your balance

Ask your agent:

```text
what's my credit balance?
```

It returns your balance and recent usage.
